READ THIS ALOUD. Record in Voice Memos. 25 to 35 seconds.

Before you start:
  Quiet room, no fan, no music.
  Mic about a hand's width away, slightly off to the side.
  One continuous take. If you fumble, start over rather than splice.
  Read it a bit faster and more direct than you normally talk.
  This is the pace and energy every video will inherit, so read it
  the way you want the voiceover to sound. Do not perform it.

Read once to warm up. Record the second pass.

Most of these tools do one thing,

and the README buries it under three paragraphs of throat clearing.

This one does not.

You point it at a folder, it reads every file,

and it tells you what is actually slow.

The project claims a 40 percent speedup.

I ran it against version 2 point 4 on my own machine

and got closer to half that.

Still worth the install.

It is a single binary, no runtime,

and it does not phone home.
