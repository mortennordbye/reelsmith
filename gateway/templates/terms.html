Terms of service

Last updated 27 August 2026.

These terms cover The Nightly Build, an automated publishing service that posts short videos about open source developer tools to @thenightlybuild on Instagram, @thenightlybuild on YouTube and @thenightlybuild on TikTok and The Nightly Build on Facebook.

The software is open source and you can read exactly what it does in this repository.

Contact: thenightlybuild@nordbye.it

What this is, and who it is for

This is not a product you sign up for. It is one operator's own publishing tool, used to post to accounts that operator owns. There is no account to create, no subscription, nothing to pay, and no way for anyone else to connect their own social media accounts to it.

If you are reading this because you found it linked from a platform's developer listing, that is why it exists: the platforms require a terms of service URL for any application that uses their APIs, including one whose only user is the person who wrote it.

Using the accounts

You are welcome to watch, follow, comment on and share the videos. Doing so is governed by the terms of whichever platform you are on, not by this document.

If you comment a keyword on an Instagram post, you may receive one automated private reply. Replies are automated and are not written by a person at the time you receive them. What is stored when that happens, and how to have it deleted, is set out in the privacy policy.

What is published

Videos describe publicly available open source repositories and link to them. They are written from public information, are automatically generated, and are narrated by a synthetic voice cloned from the operator's own recording of themselves.

They may be wrong. A video is a short summary of a project written without that project's involvement, and nothing in it is an endorsement by, or a statement from, that project or its maintainers. Treat a video as a pointer to a repository, not as documentation of it. If a video is inaccurate about a project you maintain, write to the address above and it will be corrected or removed.

Trade marks, logos and screenshots belonging to the projects covered remain theirs and are shown to identify the project being discussed.

Availability, and the absence of promises

The service runs on privately operated hardware as a personal project. It can stop, lose data, publish nothing for a week, or be shut down permanently at any time and without notice. Nothing here is a commitment to publish anything, on any schedule, or to keep anything online.
