reelsmith

An automated short video publishing service.

Every night this service picks a subject, writes a short script about it, narrates it, renders a vertical video and posts it. Roughly 45 seconds, no hype, aimed at adults who have seen a thousand generated videos.

Where it posts

To accounts on Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and Facebook, all of them owned by the operator. It never posts anywhere else and it cannot be pointed at an account it has not been given credentials for.

How it works

A scheduled job ranks candidate subjects, writes and renders a video, and adds it to a queue. This service holds that queue and publishes from it on a timetable. Anything queued can be cancelled before its slot fires.

The whole thing is open source, including this page. You can read exactly what it does in the repository.

Nobody signs up for this

It is one operator's publishing tool for accounts that operator owns. There is no account to create and no way to connect your own social media to it. The privacy policy and terms exist because the platforms require them for any application using their APIs, including one whose only user is the person who wrote it.
