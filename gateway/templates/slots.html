No days ticked means every day. A named zone rather than an offset, so the slot stays at the same local hour across a daylight saving change. The jitter also avoids landing on :00, :15, :30 and :45, and lands on a second rather than on the minute, because a post that arrives at exactly 18:00:00 every evening is the cheapest thing in the world to spot.

Slots that should survive a redeploy belong in GATEWAY_SLOTS instead, one per line, for example 18:00 Europe/Oslo jitter=15. Those show as config and are rewritten on every boot.
