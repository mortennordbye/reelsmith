Last updated 27 August 2026.

These terms cover **The Nightly Build**, an automated publishing service that
posts short videos about open source developer tools to
[@thenightlybuild](https://www.instagram.com/thenightlybuild/) on Instagram,
[@thenightlybuild](https://www.youtube.com/@thenightlybuild) on YouTube and
[@thenightlybuild](https://www.tiktok.com/@thenightlybuild) on TikTok.

The software is open source and you can read exactly what it does in
[this repository](https://github.com/mortennordbye/reelsmith).

**This is not a product you sign up for.** It is one operator's own publishing
tool, used to post to accounts that operator owns. There is no account to
create, no subscription, nothing to pay, and no way for anyone else to connect
their own social media accounts to it.

## Using the accounts

You are welcome to watch, follow, comment on and share the videos. Doing so is
governed by the terms of whichever platform you are on, not by this document.

If you comment a keyword on an Instagram post, you may receive one automated
private reply. **Replies are automated and are not written by a person at the
time you receive them.** What is stored when that happens, and how to have it
deleted, is set out in the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## What is published

Videos describe publicly available open source repositories and link to them.
They are written from public information, are automatically generated, and are
narrated by a synthetic voice cloned from the operator's own recording of
themselves.

**They may be wrong.** A video is a short summary of a project written without
that project's involvement, and nothing in it is an endorsement by, or a
statement from, that project or its maintainers. Treat a video as a pointer to a
repository, not as documentation of it. If a video is inaccurate about a project
you maintain, write to the address above and it will be corrected or removed.

Trade marks, logos and screenshots belonging to the projects covered remain
theirs and are shown to identify the project being discussed.
