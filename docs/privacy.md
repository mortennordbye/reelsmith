# Privacy policy

Last updated 27 August 2026.

This policy covers **The Nightly Build**, an automated publishing service that
posts short videos about open source developer tools to
[@thenightlybuild](https://www.instagram.com/thenightlybuild/) on Instagram,
[@thenightlybuild](https://www.youtube.com/@thenightlybuild) on YouTube and
[@thenightlybuild](https://www.tiktok.com/@thenightlybuild) on TikTok. It also
covers the comment and direct message assistant attached to the Instagram
account, which is described below and is currently dormant.

The software is open source and you can read exactly what it does in
[this repository](https://github.com/mortennordbye/reelsmith).

Contact: **thenightlybuild@nordbye.it**

## Who this affects

**If you simply watch the videos, this service holds nothing about you at all.**
It reads totals, not people. The sections below matter only if you have
commented or messaged the Instagram account.

## What the service does

Two separate things, and it is worth keeping them apart.

**It publishes.** On a timetable, it uploads a finished video to one of the
three accounts above and posts it. Everything it publishes is written and
rendered ahead of time. No part of this reads anything about the people who
watch.

**It answers comments, in principle.** On Instagram it can reply to a keyword
comment with one automated private message offering a link. **Nothing advertises
that keyword any more**, so in practice the feature is off; the code is left
connected rather than deleted, and it is described here because it is capable of
running rather than because it is running.

**Replies are automated, not written by a person at the time you receive them.**

## What it stores about people

Only what the comment and message feature needs, and only for people who used
it:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your Instagram-scoped user ID | To send you the reply you asked for and to recognise you if you write again. This is an ID scoped to this app, not your Instagram username or account ID. |
| The ID of the comment you left | So you are never sent the same automated reply twice. Instagram permits exactly one private reply per comment. |
| Whether you follow the account | Read at the moment you message, because the link is offered to followers. |
| Timestamps | When you last messaged, and whether the link was sent. |

## What it stores about posts

Separately from the above, the service records how each of its own videos
performed: view counts, reach, saves, shares, comment counts, watch time and the
share of viewers who scrolled past in the first few seconds. On TikTok this is
views, likes, comments and shares.

**These are totals about a video, not records about a person.** They arrive
already aggregated from Instagram, YouTube and TikTok, they identify nobody, and
they are used to decide what the next video should be about.

## What it does not store

- **The content of your messages.** Inbound message text is never written to
  storage. The service reacts to the fact that you wrote, not to what you said.
- **The text of your comments.** Comments are read to check for the keyword and
  are not retained.
- Your email address, phone number, real name, location, or any profile field
  beyond your username and follower relationship.
- Anything at all about viewers who do not comment or message.

There is no analytics, no advertising, no profiling, and no tracking across
sites. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

## Who it is shared with

Nobody. The only third parties involved are the platforms themselves, because
posts and messages necessarily travel over their APIs and each one processes
them as the operator of its own service:

- [Meta's privacy policy](https://www.instagram.com/legal/privacy/), for Instagram
- [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), for YouTube
- [TikTok's privacy policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/eea/privacy-policy/en), for TikTok

The service runs on hardware operated privately by the account owner, not on a
third-party cloud, and the data described above never leaves it.

## How long it is kept

Conversation records are kept while the account operates, so that you are not
re-sent an automated message you already received. Ask for deletion at any time
and it is removed. Post performance figures are kept indefinitely, because they
describe the account's own videos rather than any person.

## Deleting your data

Email **thenightlybuild@nordbye.it** from any address, or send a direct message
to the Instagram account, saying you want your data deleted. Include your handle
so the record can be found. It will be deleted within 30 days, usually sooner.

Blocking the account, or deleting your comment, also stops any further automated
message. It does not by itself erase the stored ID, so ask if you want that gone.

## Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, the GDPR applies. You may request access to
what is held about you, correction, deletion, or a copy in a portable form, and
you may object to the processing. Use the contact address above. The legal basis
is legitimate interest in answering a message you initiated, and you can end it
at any time by asking.

## Children

The account is aimed at professional software developers, and the service is not
directed at anyone under 13.
